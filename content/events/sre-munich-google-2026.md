## Speakers & Abstract:
### Talk 1: The second edition of the SRE Book: What has changed in the last decade
**Christof Leng / Google**
Google's SRE Book is widely considered "the bible" of Site Reliability Engineering. In 2026 we published a completely rewritten second edition of the book. While the core principles of SRE have stood the rest of time, Google and the industry have learned many things since the publication of the first edition in 2016. This talk gives an overview of what to expect from the new book and how the SRE discipline has evolved in the last ten years.

**Speaker Bio:**
Christof Leng has been working as a Site Reliability Engineer for Google for more than 11 years in the Dublin and Munich offices. He is an author and editor for the second edition of "Site Reliability Engineering: How Google Runs Production Systems". He has worked on systems in Google's ads, cloud, and internal developer infrastructure and has been building and managing infrastructure and has various teams in these areas over the years. He has been responsible for central Google SRE programs like the SRE engagement model, production excellence (ProdEx), and production launch reviews. Christof holds a PhD in computer Science from TU Darmstadt and has been a postdoc at ICSI and UC Berkeley. He has been a vice president for the German Informatics Society (GI). Christof lives in Darmstadt with his wife, three children, and two cats.

### Talk 2: Ask your Cluster in Slack: Building an SRE Agent with HolmesGPT
**Kolawole Akinwande / EGYM**
Engineers at EGYM now investigate alerts by mentioning an in-cluster SRE agent in Slack. It reads Kubernetes state, Prometheus metrics, service inventory, incidents, and runbooks, then answers in the alert thread. This talk covers how we built egym-sre-agent on HolmesGPT: runbooks as markdown skills grounded in our SRE docs, and read-only guardrails that prevent it from changing infrastructure. We’ll also share the trade-offs we made, and what we’d change next.

**Speaker Bio:**
Kolawole Akinwande is a Site Reliability Engineer at EGYM with over 10 years of experience in software and systems engineering. At EGYM, Kolawole builds tooling that helps engineers investigate and operate production systems, including the company’s in-cluster SRE agent. Outside work, Kolawole is a Christian, a husband, and a father to a 4-year-old son.
